Usage rules

Brand color is for progress

Use it on primary CTAs, progress bars, the wordmark and headings. Keep it off body text and small links.

Blue is for links

Links, secondary buttons, focus rings and the current nav item. Nothing else.

Feedback colors are fixed

Green means correct and red means incorrect in every brand, so a right answer always looks right.

No shadows or gradients

Depth comes from borders and the 4px lip. Surfaces stay flat paper.

Don't put small white text on brand fills

White on the brand color is fine at 15px bold caps on buttons. For anything smaller, use Night ink or Charcoal.

One primary action per view

A study screen has one obvious next step. Everything else is secondary or ghost.
